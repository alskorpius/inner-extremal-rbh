Summary (3): a regular exit from the T-region = a trajectory asymptotically approaching a fixed point (b*, ḃ = 0) (a degenerate horizon in the static picture). For a smooth autonomous system such trajectories form the stable manifold of a saddle (measure zero) or a degenerate point (triple root: coefficient 1/b*² + 4πb*ρ'(b*) = 0) -- there is no open set, by uniqueness of solutions. A sink would require a term with ḃ in p_x (a dissipative, non-Lagrangian medium) or a non-smooth right-hand side.
