  ℓ₀ = 10.7 km: extremality boundary of the triple-root family lambda_ext = 1.066 → M = 6.80 M☉; the companion paper's criterion ℓ₀/M ≥ 1.49 (profile n = 6, without a triple root) → 4.86 M☉.
