==============================================================================
N1.  Memory (non-local in proper time): does a relaxation kernel help?
==============================================================================
Extended state (b, v, q, mu):   mu' = (D - mu)/tau_r,   p_perp = w(rho) rho - G mu,
with the drive D taken to be the degeneracy defect q itself (the sharpest choice --
the memory is fed exactly the quantity that must be driven to zero).  This contains
the Israel-Stewart structure T29 tested, and more: an arbitrary linear memory kernel
can be written this way.
Prediction of A3: mu enters q' = 16 pi b v (w rho - G mu) multiplied by v, so at
v = 0 its contribution to the Jacobian vanishes and the (v, q) block still has zero
trace.  The extra eigenvalue -1/tau_r contracts only the memory direction.
  tau_r = 0.05  G = +0.3: rrrrrrr   degenerate 0/7
  tau_r = 0.05  G = +1.0: rrrrrrr   degenerate 0/7
  tau_r = 0.05  G = +3.0: rrrrrrr   degenerate 0/7
  tau_r = 0.05  G = -1.0: nnnnnnn   degenerate 0/7
  tau_r = 0.3   G = +0.3: rrrrrrr   degenerate 0/7
  tau_r = 0.3   G = +1.0: rrrrrrr   degenerate 0/7
  tau_r = 0.3   G = +3.0: rrrrrrr   degenerate 0/7
  tau_r = 0.3   G = -1.0: nnnnnnn   degenerate 0/7
  tau_r = 1.0   G = +0.3: rrrrrrr   degenerate 0/7
  tau_r = 1.0   G = +1.0: rrrrrrr   degenerate 0/7
  tau_r = 1.0   G = +3.0: rrrrrrr   degenerate 0/7
  tau_r = 1.0   G = -1.0: nnnnnnn   degenerate 0/7
  Not one of the twelve memory laws produces a degenerate exit -- not even at eps = 0,
  where the gain G displaces the separatrix away from the tuned profile.  Non-locality
  in TIME does not defeat the theorem, for the structural reason given above.  (This
  generalises T29's Israel-Stewart runs, which used one kernel and two relaxation
  times, to an arbitrary single-pole memory kernel and both signs of the gain.)

==============================================================================
N2.  Non-local in r: the medium reads the TOTAL mass and sets ell = lambda M
==============================================================================
This is the project's tracking law (ratchet).  It is non-local: M = m(infinity) is
an integral of rho over the whole profile, not a function of the fields at a point,
so it is outside the premises of the theorem -- and it works, trivially, because a
homothety (m -> A m, r -> A r) leaves f invariant.  A tracker that is EXACT keeps the
triple root for every mass.  The question is what an inexact tracker costs.

  (a) exact tracker, ell = lambda M, for a range of masses A:

      M = 0.9  : inner root at r = 0.609418 = 0.677131 M, kappa_- = -7.89e-07, kappa_- M = -7.099e-07, sign changes of f = 15
      M = 1.0  : inner root at r = 0.677131 = 0.677131 M, kappa_- = -7.10e-07, kappa_- M = -7.099e-07, sign changes of f = 15
      M = 1.1  : inner root at r = 0.744844 = 0.677131 M, kappa_- = -6.45e-07, kappa_- M = -7.099e-07, sign changes of f = 15
      M = 2.0  : inner root at r = 1.354261 = 0.677131 M, kappa_- = -3.55e-07, kappa_- M = -7.099e-07, sign changes of f = 15
      M = 10.0 : inner root at r = 6.771306 = 0.677131 M, kappa_- = -7.10e-08, kappa_- M = -7.099e-07, sign changes of f = 15

      2 m_b'(r), and the degeneracy condition 2 m_b'(R_-) = 1 makes it exactly 1:
      delta f = eta * g with g = +1, the same |g| as T1's mass-amplitude case (g_a = -1).

      CONSEQUENCE.  kappa_-(eta) is T1's law with the SAME exponent AND the same
      coefficient.  T1's requirement eps <~ 3e-32 for a 10 M_sun hole becomes
      eta <~ 3e-32 on the relative tracking error, with no gain at all.
      The non-local law removes the tuning of the profile only by requiring an
      equally exact tuning of the tracker.  It is a counterexample to the theorem as
      stated, and NOT a physical mechanism: the enumeration did not miss anything.
      Its independent price is already on record: accretion_tracking (an ingoing flux
      of negative energy up to 0.2 Mdot inside R < 1.49 M, or emission by the medium
      towards R_-) and NONLOCAL_LAW_REPORT / inhomogeneous_collapse section 3.4 (the
      local version ell = lambda m_enc(r) gives a singular centre, K ~ 1e24...1e29).

==============================================================================
SUMMARY of the non-local tests
==============================================================================
Locality is the load-bearing premise, but in a precise and narrow sense: what fails
is not 'non-locality in time' (memory laws are still covered, N1) but dependence on
a FUNCTIONAL OF THE PROFILE, specifically the total mass (N2).  And the escape is
not free: an exact tracker is needed, with the same 10^-32 accuracy T1 demanded of
the profile itself.  The theorem is therefore sharp and the loophole is empty of
physics -- which is the honest way to state it.
